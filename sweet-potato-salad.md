# Sweet Potato Salad

Ingredients as Josh dictated them, November 2026. No method was written down — this is the ingredient list for the salad he makes.

## Ingredients

- 6 medium sweet potatoes, cubed
- 6 hard-boiled eggs, chopped
- 2 green apples, chopped with the skin still on
- 10 strips bacon, roughly chopped

## Dressing

- 8 Tbsp olive oil
- 2 Tbsp chives, chopped
- 8 Tbsp paleo mayonnaise
- 4 Tbsp fresh lemon juice
- 2 Tbsp Dijon or homemade mustard
- Sea salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste

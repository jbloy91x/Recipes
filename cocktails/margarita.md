# Margarita

A classic margarita on the rocks.

## Ingredients (1 serving)
- 1 1/2 oz silver (blanco) tequila
- 1 oz orange liqueur (Cointreau, Grand Marnier, or triple sec)
- 3/4 oz freshly squeezed lime juice
- 1/4 oz agave syrup or simple syrup (optional, for extra sweetness)
- Coarse salt and a lime wedge (optional, for rimming and garnish)
- Ice

## Instructions
1. Prep the glass: Rub a lime wedge along half or all of the glass rim, then dip it into coarse salt. Fill the glass with fresh ice.
2. Shake: Add the tequila, orange liqueur, lime juice, and optional sweetener to a cocktail shaker filled with ice. Shake vigorously for about 10–15 seconds until well chilled.
3. Strain and garnish: Strain the drink over fresh ice in your prepared glass and garnish with a lime wedge.

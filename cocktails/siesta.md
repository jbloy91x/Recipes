# Siesta

A tequila and Campari cocktail, from Josh's handwritten recipe tag (Nov 2026).

## Ingredients
- 2 oz tequila (or mezcal)
- 1 oz Campari
- 1 oz lime juice
- 1/2 oz simple syrup ("SS" on the tag)

## Method
Shake with ice, then strain and serve.

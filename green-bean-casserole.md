# Green Bean Casserole (Classic Campbell's)

Source: [Allrecipes — Campbell's Green Bean Casserole](https://www.allrecipes.com/recipe/151595/campbells-green-bean-casserole/)
The classic Thanksgiving version: cream of mushroom soup, green beans, and crispy fried onions.

## Ingredients

- 1 can (10.5 oz) Campbell's condensed cream of mushroom soup
- ½ cup milk
- 1 tsp soy sauce
- Dash of black pepper
- 4 cups cooked cut green beans
- 1⅓ cups French's French Fried Onions, divided

## Method

1. Heat oven to 350°.
2. Stir the soup, milk, soy sauce, pepper, beans, and ⅔ cup of the fried onions together in a 1½-qt casserole dish.
3. Bake 25 minutes, then top with the remaining fried onions and bake 5 minutes more, until golden.

## Notes

- Green beans: 4 cups cooked — about two 14.5 oz cans drained, a 16–20 oz bag frozen, or 1½ lb fresh (blanch first).
- Doubling: for a 3-qt dish, double everything — 2 cans soup, 1 cup milk, 2 tsp soy sauce, 8 cups beans, 2⅔ cups onions.

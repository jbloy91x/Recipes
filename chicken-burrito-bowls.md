# Chicken Burrito Bowls (Meal Prep)

Josh's protein-forward lunch meal prep, November 2026. Makes 5 lunches.

## Ingredients

- 2.5 lbs chicken breast
- 2 cans black beans
- Quinoa
- Bell peppers
- Red onion
- Corn
- Salsa
- Lime
- Greek yogurt

## Josh's actual batch

- 23 oz chicken + 880 g black beans (cooked with olive oil), split into 5 lunches.

## Macros (per lunch, from his amounts)

- ~495 cal · ~56g protein · ~42g carbs · ~15g fiber · ~11g fat
- Whole batch: ~2,475 cal · ~280g protein

Assumptions: chicken is cooked chicken breast; olive oil about 2 Tbsp total for the batch.

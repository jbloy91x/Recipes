# Lemon-Oregano Chicken with Roasted Vegetables

From the Mediterranean meal-prep options, October 2026 — the one Josh picked. Meal prep for two; keeps 3–4 days in the fridge, and the lemon keeps it from tasting tired on reheat.

## Ingredients

- ~2.5 lbs boneless skinless chicken thighs
- 3 lemons (juice of 2, third sliced for roasting)
- 1 head garlic, minced (about 6 cloves)
- 2 tsp dried oregano
- 1/3 cup olive oil
- 3 zucchini, half-moons
- 2 red onions, wedged
- 1 pint cherry tomatoes
- Salt and pepper

## Method

1. Whisk lemon juice, garlic, oregano, olive oil, salt, and pepper. Toss with the chicken and marinate 30 minutes (or up to overnight).
2. Heat oven to 425°F. Spread zucchini, onions, tomatoes, and lemon slices on a sheet pan, drizzle with oil, salt, and pepper.
3. Nestle the chicken among the vegetables. Roast 25–30 minutes, until the chicken hits 165°F and the veg are browned at the edges.
4. Rest 5 minutes, then slice or portion.

Serve over rice or quinoa.

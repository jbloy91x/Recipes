# Simple-Is-Best Stuffing

Source: [Bon Appétit](https://www.bonappetit.com/recipe/simple-is-best-stuffing-dressing) — Victoria Granof, November 2012
Yield: 8–10 servings · Total time: about 2 hours 45 minutes · 4.6 stars (497 reviews)

Classic Thanksgiving stuffing: buttery torn bread, sautéed onions and celery, fresh parsley, sage, rosemary, and thyme. Crisp golden top, tender custardy middle.

## Ingredients

- ¾ cup (1½ sticks) unsalted butter, plus more for the baking dish
- 1 lb good-quality day-old white bread, torn into 1" pieces (about 10 cups)
- 2½ cups chopped yellow onions
- 1½ cups celery, sliced ¼"
- ½ cup chopped flat-leaf parsley
- 2 Tbsp chopped fresh sage
- 1 Tbsp chopped fresh rosemary
- 1 Tbsp chopped fresh thyme
- 2 tsp Diamond Crystal kosher salt (or 1 tsp Morton)
- 1 tsp freshly ground black pepper
- 2½ cups low-sodium chicken broth, divided
- 2 large eggs

## Method

1. Heat oven to 250°. Butter a 13x9x2" baking dish. Spread the torn bread in a single layer on a rimmed baking sheet and bake, stirring occasionally, until dried out, about 1 hour. Cool and move to a very large bowl.
2. Melt the butter in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the onions and celery and cook, stirring often, until just beginning to brown, about 10 minutes. Add to the bread bowl, then stir in the parsley, sage, rosemary, thyme, salt, and pepper. Drizzle in 1¼ cups broth and toss gently. Let cool.
3. Heat oven to 350°. Whisk the eggs with the remaining 1¼ cups broth, fold into the bread mixture until combined, and transfer to the prepared dish. Cover with foil and bake until the center reads 160° on an instant-read thermometer, 35–40 minutes.
4. Uncover and bake until set and the top is golden brown and crisp, 35–45 minutes longer.

## Notes

- Bread: use a sturdy, crusty loaf (pane Pugliese, French white boule, or country-style sourdough). Skip soft sandwich bread — it turns mushy. Weigh out 1 lb before drying; the 10-cup figure is approximate. Don't skip the low-oven drying step even if the loaf is already a day old.
- Make-ahead: bake covered (before browning) up to 1 day ahead — the page's FAQ says up to 3 days — then cool, cover, and chill. On serving day, uncover and bake at 350° until crisp on top and 165° in the center, 45–60 minutes.
- Broth: homemade turkey stock works, or low-sodium chicken broth from a carton. Vegetable broth makes it vegetarian.

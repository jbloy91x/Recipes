# High Protein Stuffed Shells with Pesto

Source: [Erin Lives Whole](https://www.erinliveswhole.com/high-protein-stuffed-shells-with-pesto/)
Prep 10 min · Cook 40 min · 8 servings · ~650 cal and ~37g protein per serving

Jumbo shells stuffed with cottage cheese, turkey sausage, and spinach, baked in marinara with pesto and mozzarella.

## Ingredients

- 12 oz box jumbo shells
- 2 Tbsp olive oil
- ½ yellow onion, diced
- 1 lb ground turkey sausage
- 16 oz cottage cheese (4% milkfat)
- 1 cup shredded parmesan cheese, divided
- 5 oz spinach, wilted, squeezed dry, and chopped
- ¼ cup fresh chopped parsley, plus extra for topping
- 1 cup fresh pesto, divided
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- ½ tsp red pepper flakes
- ½ tsp salt
- ¼ tsp pepper
- 3 cups marinara sauce
- 2 cups mozzarella cheese

## Method

1. Heat oven to 375°F. Line a 9x13 dish with 1½ cups marinara.
2. Cook the shells in salted water until just shy of al dente (they finish in the oven). Drain and set aside.
3. Sauté the onion in olive oil over medium-high heat 3–4 minutes, then add the turkey sausage, breaking it up, and cook until no longer pink. Cool 10 minutes.
4. In a large bowl, mix cottage cheese, ½ cup parmesan, spinach, parsley, ½ cup pesto, garlic, oregano, red pepper flakes, salt, and pepper. Fold in the cooled sausage.
5. Fill each shell with about ¼ cup of filling and nestle into the sauced dish.
6. Pour the remaining 1½ cups marinara over the top, spoon the remaining ½ cup pesto over it, then top with mozzarella and the remaining ½ cup parmesan.
7. Bake covered with foil 20 minutes, then uncovered 10 minutes more.
8. Sprinkle with remaining parsley and rest about 15 minutes before serving.

## Notes

- Undercook the shells 1–2 minutes less than the package says so they don't tear or turn mushy.
- Squeeze the spinach really dry — watery spinach makes watery filling.
- Let the sausage cool before mixing or the cottage cheese can separate.
- Storage: fridge up to 4 days; assemble up to 24 hours ahead, or freeze unbaked up to 3 months (thaw overnight before baking).
- Josh's list used ground beef instead of turkey sausage.
